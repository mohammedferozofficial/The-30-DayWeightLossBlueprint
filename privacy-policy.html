Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2024

Introduction

The 30-Day Weight Loss Blueprint ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you purchase our digital product.

Information We Collect

Payment Information: We use PayPal as our payment processor. We do not store your credit card or financial information. PayPal handles all payment transactions securely.

Contact Information: When you make a purchase, we collect:

  • Email address (for delivery of the ebook)
  • Name (as provided through PayPal)

Order Information: We keep records of your purchase for customer service and support purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver your purchased ebook via email

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All payment processing is handled securely through PayPal's encrypted system. We do not have access to your full payment details.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics (anonymized data)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

Data Retention

We retain your email address and order information for customer support purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for website functionality and Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information.

Children's Privacy

Our product is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

If you are accessing our website from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States.

Changes to This Policy

Common Questions

Will I be charged if I leave?
No. You won't be charged unless you complete the PayPal checkout. Closing the window doesn't process any payment.
Can I try a different payment method?
Yes! PayPal accepts credit cards, debit cards, PayPal balance, and bank transfers. You can select a different method when you retry.
What if my payment was declined?
Contact your bank or card issuer to check if there's an issue. Then try again with a different payment method.
Is the ebook still $9.99?
Yes! The price hasn't changed. Go back and try checkout again.
